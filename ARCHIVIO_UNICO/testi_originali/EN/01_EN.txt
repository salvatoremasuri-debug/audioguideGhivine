GROTTA DEL BUE MARINO — AUDIO GUIDE

1. RULES

Welcome to the Grotta del Bue Marino, one of the most extraordinary places in the Gulf of Orosei.
Before beginning the tour, it is essential to know and respect the visiting rules: they are in place to protect you and this unique, fragile, and millennia-old environment.

The visit will last approximately 50 minutes.
The route runs along a single walkway, accessible to everyone, covering approximately 1 kilometre on the way in and 1 kilometre on the way back. Pushchairs are permitted and wheelchairs can be used for the entire route. The path is linear and has no branches open to the public.

It is, however, very important to respect the timing.
Within 50 minutes, you must be back outside the cave in order to board the vessel that will take you to your next destination. For this reason, it is essential to keep up with the guide's pace and not fall too far behind. Stopping for too long along the route could compromise the timing of the visit and risk missing the boat. By working together and maintaining a steady pace, everyone will be able to enjoy the experience calmly and in full.

The cave is an extremely delicate ecosystem.
Do not touch the stalactites and stalagmites: even a simple touch can interrupt a growth process that takes thousands of years.
Do not collect anything and do not leave any litter. Every element here has scientific and naturalistic value.
Follow only the walkways and the marked route.
Leaving the authorised areas is not permitted. This ensures your safety and protects the limestone formations.
Keep your voice low.
The cave amplifies sounds, and a quiet environment allows everyone to enjoy a truly evocative and authentic experience.
Smoking is not permitted.
Open flames are strictly forbidden.
Photography is allowed, but the use of camera flash is prohibited: in addition to potentially disturbing the balance of the underground environment, the flash compromises the quality of the visit itself. In a naturally dark space like the cave, sudden artificial light alters the atmosphere, creates strong visual pollution, and breaks the spell of shadows, silence, and reflections on the water that makes the experience unique.

The guide is responsible for your safety.
Listen carefully to the instructions, especially in the narrower sections or where the ground is wet.
The Grotta del Bue Marino is a precious natural heritage site: your respect is an integral part of the experience.

Thank you for your cooperation and enjoy your visit.
